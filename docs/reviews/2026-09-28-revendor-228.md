## 5. Schema findings

**(a) The brief's premise does not hold at the tree.** The brief says Aurora's clip-manifest reader is a closed
schema that refuses unknown keys. `src/core/formats/donors/clip-manifest-doc.ts` is the opposite by design ("NOTHING
IS DROPPED ON WRITE"): it keeps the RAW object, appends a clip, and preserves every other key. So `y_from`, `y_why`,
`fill` and `shafts` break no reader.
- Measured on the re-captured `paste-music.cases.json`: `paste-music-r3.test.ts`, which holds that `withClip`'s output
  IS the manifest aeon judged, is green over the new manifests with `y_from`/`y_why` in them.
- `bg_blobs` is a key under `crossing_overrides` in the two woven manifests (`s2_woven`, `s2_woven_2d`). At the pin
  it is read only by aeon's `tools/clip_rom_bake.py`, and it is in no clips.json that Aurora vendors.
  `grep -rn 'crossing_overrides\|bg_blobs' src` finds nothing, so the raw-keeping writer carries it through untouched.
- Nothing needed adding to any schema, and no key was deleted from any fixture.

**(b) BLOCKED: the donor page's pool readout on a woven act (shafts or fill).**
`src/core/formats/donors/clipact-pool.ts` reads `pool.per_clip` and `pool.per_corridor` only. It checks aeon's
invariant as `sum(tiles_added over per_clip + per_corridor) + 1 == pool.tiles` (line 109). At the pin, aeon
(`clip_act_bake.py` `PER_CLIP_POOL_FIELDS` and its emission) states that invariant over **per_clip, per_corridor,
per_shaft and per_fill**.

**Measured, not argued.** I ran `python3 tools/clip_act_bake.py bake games/sonic4/data/clips/s2_woven/clips.json
--out ../bake_s2_woven --json` in the copy (exit 0) and fed its `clipact.json` to Aurora's `readPoolRows`, in a
one-off vitest file that was deleted afterwards.
- aeon's file: `pool.tiles` 2894; `tiles_added` sums to 2858 over 8 clips, 26 over 4 corridors, 9 over 7 shafts and
  0 over 1 fill. 2858 + 26 + 9 + 0 + 1 = 2894, so aeon's invariant holds.
- Aurora's answer: `state: present`, 8 clips and 4 corridors, and
  `broken: ["the rows' added tiles plus the blank make 2885, but pool.tiles is 2894"]`.
- `DonorPasteSection.tsx` shows `broken` to the author as "aeon's own sums disagree in this file". On any act with a
  shaft or a fill that adds a tile, that tells the author aeon is wrong when it is not, and the 7 shaft and 1 fill
  rows are not shown at all.
- The page was not driven in the app (no runtime here).

This is a design choice, not a transcription, so I stopped. Two things need deciding:
- how the page shows shaft and fill rows (their own section, or folded in with corridors; a "fill" row has no rect);
- whether the invariant should count rows the page does not show.

Widening the sum without showing the rows would hide 9 tiles' provenance. Showing them is a layout call. Neither was
built. The current fixtures carry no shaft or fill, so no suite row can see this. A woven `clipact.json` fixture
would be the red-first row for whoever takes it.

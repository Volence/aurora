### What the app wrote

```json
  "sceneRef": "ojz_act1_sec7_worldwater" }
```

`games/sonic4/data/editor/effects/ojz_act1_sec7_worldwater.json` — three strips:

| strip | top | fa / fb | carries | why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 0 | `1_8` / `1_8` | — | sky / far bank; a still backdrop is what makes the water read as moving |
| 1 | **3** | `1` / `1_2` | `curve → FACTOR_1_4` | **the surface** — channel 2's band top |
| 2 | **162** | `3_4` / `1_2` | `vsplit.at = 67`, `curve → FACTOR_1_8` | **underwater** — channel 3's band top, and the split itself |

And it reaches the engine through the real constructors:

```
pub const Scene_Editor_ojz_act1_sec7_worldwater: Scene = scene(
    layers: [ layer(world_y: 0, fa: FACTOR_1_8, fb: FACTOR_1_8),
              layer(world_y: 3, fa: FACTOR_1, fb: FACTOR_1_2, curve: SceneCurve.To(FACTOR_1_4)),
              layer(world_y: 162, fa: FACTOR_3_4, fb: FACTOR_1_2,
                    vsplit: SceneVSplit.At(67), curve: SceneCurve.To(FACTOR_1_8)), … ],
    count: 3, v_factor: 15)
```

### Design calls, and the reasoning

- **The strip tops are the bands' own screen lines (3 and 162), not the world
  anchors 4320/4410.** The control is labelled `Screen line (0..511) — a plane
  line; the scene is locked`: at `v_factor` 15 (the LOCKED sentinel) the layer top
  is in *screen* space. Typing a world Y there is the exact hazard the panel warns
  about — it "lands 256 times down the level, validates clean, and the band
  silently never appears". aeon's sidecar declares its bands in **screen lines,
  1:1**, so scene and bands end up in one coordinate system. The world anchors are
  `patch_world_ys` on the **preset**, not the scene (see §4).
- **Parallax is slower below the split than at it** (`3_4` vs `1`). The medium
  change is sold by the two halves disagreeing about how fast the world goes past;
  slower-below reads as heavier water rather than as a broken camera.
- **`curve` carries the depth, on both feature strips.** Forced, then endorsed:
  see §4 for why deform was impossible. The curve gives Plane B something to vary
  down the strip, which is the honest substitute for the compression `rowRemap`
  would have given.
- `TASTE` — the exact factor ladder (`1_8` sky, `1` surface, `3_4` underwater) and
  the curve targets (`1_4`, `1_8`) are my judgement, not derived. Screenshots are
  in `docs/captures/2026-09-05-sec7-scene/`. Easy to retune; nothing else depends
  on the specific rungs.

### The line budget

158 + 62 + 1 = **221**, exactly full. Nothing here tries to widen a band; the two
strips sit at the two bands' tops and the budget is untouched.

## 4. What the UI could NOT express — the four gaps

Each was found by **a build refusing**, not by reading source, and **none was
worked around by hand-editing the JSON** — doing so would have destroyed the only
evidence that the control was missing.

**(a) `layer.dsa` / `layer.dsb` — no control at all.** Confirmed on screen (row
`[7b]`: zero matching inputs or selects). The panel's own source says *"The card
has no control for dsa/dsb/phase, so this state arrives from a HAND-EDITED file."*
The dispatch asked for these; the honest answer is the UI cannot express them.

**(b) `scene.anchor` — no control, and no writer of any kind.** There is no
command in `src/core/editing/commands.ts` and nothing in the renderer that writes
it. This is the one that hurts: aeon's `scene()` refuses a `rowRemap` layer
without `anchor: SceneAnchor.At(ch, dsa, dsb)` (design §9.1 precondition 2). So
**`rowRemap` — whose own tooltip names "Hydrocity's waterline", exactly this
scene — is authorable in the panel but never buildable from the editor alone.**
`CAP_ROW_REMAP` *is* declared by sonic4, so this is purely the missing writer.

**(c) Deform is unreachable for sonic4 by every route.** Proved by three
successive refusals:

1. `deform: Own(..)` is refused unless the scene *also* attaches a plane-shared
   table — `own()` overrides, it cannot *be* the only table, or every other band
   samples from address 0.
2. Attaching both folds **`CAP_MULTI_DEFORM_TABLE` (`$0020`)** into the scene mask
   — and sonic4's `SCANLINE_CAPS` is **`$0FDE`**, which does not declare that bit.
   Build-fatal.
3. Which leaves a shared table alone — and a layer only samples it with a live
   `dsa`/`dsb`, which is gap (a).

Every route needs either a capability sonic4 lacks or a control Aurora lacks.
Widening `SCANLINE_CAPS` is an engine decision in `game.emp` and was not mine to
make, so the scene ships without deform and says so.

**(d) `patch_motion[2]`/`[3]` are not a scene key at all.** They live on the
**preset** document (Colour tab, bound by `rasterRef`), while this parcel's binding
is `sceneRef`. Section 7's preset is aeon's own `OJZ_Preset_Sec7` `.emp` record and
the dispatch says no `.emp` edit is needed — so making the edges move on their own
is a *separate binding*, not a field this scene forgot.

> Also worth knowing: `layer()` refuses `curve` **and** `deform` on one strip —
> *"the fill's curve loop already spends all seven usable data registers … and a
> sampled channel needs three more."* A hardware budget, not a rule to argue with.

### Is section 7 bindable from the UI now?

**Yes, for `sceneRef` — I bound it, through the Section assignment control**
(rows `[6a]`/`[6b]`), and the app wrote the sidecar. Relevant to the cold read's

`null`, so it *is* available for an Aurora preset — but its raster program is
aeon's `OJZ_Preset_Sec7`, so binding an Aurora preset there would be a
substitution, not a free slot.

## 7. Rig faults that would have faked a result

- **The borrowed build tree.** The first run reported `BORROWED AND DRIFTED: 4
  source file(s) differ` — it had walked up and was driving the **main checkout's**
  `dist/`, without my step-1 edits. Every row was `no-element`, so nothing was
  carried forward, but a subtler drift would have produced a confident green about
  the wrong bytes. Fixed by making the worktree genuinely runnable
  (`node_modules/.bin/electron` does not exist in a worktree) and the harness now
  **refuses** on `RUN.borrowed` and on a missing `dist/main/index.mjs` — the
  resolver returns a path whether or not anything is there, which otherwise
  surfaces 45 s later as the misleading *"CDP target never appeared"*. The final
  run reports `pinned: AURORA_BUILT_TREE=<worktree>`.
- **`no-sub-tab` was navigation, not a missing control.** The sub-tab bar is not
  rendered until the Effects tab is open, so the selector legitimately matched
  nothing. Reading it the other way would have written up the panel as broken.
- **Guessed selectors.** Several controls are not named after their keys — the
  layer top is `Screen line`, `deform.own`'s option *values* are `none`/`on` while
  `own` is only the label, and the section picker has no
  `[data-effects-section-picker]` in the DOM. A `DUMP_TITLES` census settled all of
  it, and is kept so a future `no-element` means *absent* rather than *my regex was
  wrong*. Title regexes are prefix-matched with `\b` and never end-anchored.
- **Cross-run state pollution.** Two rows failed on a re-run because the clone
  still held the previous run's scene. The clone is reset to pristine before each
  run; both rows compare against a **before** reading rather than a bare
  `exists()`.

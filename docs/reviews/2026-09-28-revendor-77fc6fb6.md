## 3. What aeon changed

`f8796a92` adds **K11**. A corridor can now carry an optional `path_lines: [{mouth, donor_obj03: {x, y}, why}]` entry,
one per mouth. The loader checks only its shape:
- the keys must be exactly those three;
- `mouth` must be east or west, and a clip must meet the corridor at that mouth;
- no mouth may appear twice;
- `x` and `y` must be integers;
- `why` must be a non-empty string.

K11 refusals carry the corridor's own subject (`kind: "corridor"`).

`Corridor.as_json` writes `path_lines` only when a corridor has some. In `s2_woven`, the `mtz_to_cpz` corridor declares
one line at its east mouth: the donor Obj03 at (6536, 1152).
